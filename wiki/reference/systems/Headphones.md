**Summary:** Sony headphones amplify sharp sounds (the BP garage beep, a toy's click) even with cancellation on; Apple's don't. Cleaning the mics restores cancellation. SomniPods 3 noted as side-sleeping sleep buds, with an app paywall complaint.

Related: [[Sleep]] · [[Household and admin#Nightclub noise log]] · [[Mac]]

# Sony
Sony headphones had issues with strong sounds.

The inner ears made the beep at BP garage much louder - this was with sound enhanced but it was just way too loud.
The normal headphones make the clicking sound from the toy much louder - with sound cancelling


# Apple
Apples earphones don't have that issue sony had.

If the sound cancellation gets worse, make sure you properly clean them all properly, the speakers and all the mics. 

Weird noises when scrolling reddit, maybe another reddit screen playing a video, open separately on youtube will get it working without the stuttering style sounds.

# SomniPods 3

Supposed to be good sleep/noise cancelling earbuds that you can sleep on your side with.
Some users couldn't work out how to use it without paying for the app.
