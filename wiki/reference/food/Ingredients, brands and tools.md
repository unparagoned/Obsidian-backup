# Canned tomatoes

Mutti is a good brand — no citric acid and no BPA in the lining. Might want passata in a jar rather than a can.

Biona Organic is good as well. Mr Organic is good — no BPA lining.

# Fish worth avoiding

- **Swordfish** — hard to cook. Dry and fishy.
- **Sea bream** — the HelloFresh one is a bit more fishy than other fish. Google says it's not supposed to be that fishy.
