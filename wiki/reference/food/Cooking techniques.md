# Steak

Sous vide. Cool before searing. Sear at 204°C or lower — don't need a really hot pan.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZY8xbdHfWk

# Salmon

Source: "I Tested 4 Salmon Types, Only One Wins" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZTvUsHzf2o

- Oil and salt all over
- Score three lines down the skin side
- Cook on medium-low heat around 3 min till it's a third of the way through
- Flip, turn heat to low, add butter and baste
- Scottish and Atlantic are good. King is good as well but very soft.

# Tuna steak

Yellowfin is medium in terms of mercury and has good taste.

Ingredients: chilli pepper · sesame seed oil · half a bottle of sweet chilli sauce · ginger · Chinese 5 spice · 2 limes/lemons · tuna steak.

Chop up and mix all ingredients. Get the pan very hot — 14 was too hot. Cook no more than 30-45s each side.

Sides: frozen veg, jacket potato.

# Vegetables

#vegetables Source: "7 Techniques to INSTANTLY Upgrade Your Vegetables" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a8mRGkGlLJA

- **Carrots** #carrots — cook in stock and honey, then when reduced add butter to emulsify.
- **Brussels sprouts** #brusselsprouts — high temperature to brown with a lot of oil, then cover and steam on low; add lemon then parmesan.
- **Cauliflower** #cauliflower — high temp to brown, then into the oven.
- **Beetroot** — lots of oil and salt on whole beets, wrap in a tinfoil package, then oven; take the peel off with kitchen roll, cut up, add olive oil, balsamic vinegar and salt.
- **Kale** #kale — high temp and lots of oil, add garlic, add chicken stock with the lid on.

# Roasted shallots

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/efQgC4jI2Is

Halve. Fry for a bit. Oven at 200°C till soft. Sprinkle caster sugar on the cut side. Fry till caramelised. Add butter, flip, add stock. Baste with stock; add thyme, salt and pepper.

# Tofu

So tasteless and plain — try and add flavour.

Tried: Worcestershire sauce, sesame sauce, garlic, ginger, soaked for 1 hour; then cornflour, flour, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, pepper. Sounds good, but definitely need to put way more spices in — it was still very plain.

# Fish fingers (air fryer)

# Birds Eye frozen chicken (air fryer)

2 breasts for 4 min, then add frozen veg and run for another 4 min. Add garlic/ginger to a bowl with olive oil, balsamic vinegar, salt and pepper — add the veg to that to coat.

Pan crust at 6 min was too much: a bit dry, temp ~100°C. Maybe try 4min30s but measure temp. Pan crust with frozen veg on the outside: 4:30 wasn't long enough, 8:15 was.

**Chicken Kiev** — pan crust 10 min was way overcooked; the bottom completely burnt and the tip burnt as well, temp was ~90°C, so maybe 7-8 min. Small Kiev: 5 min was too short; 6 min was good, though the middle was only around 60°C, so give it a few minutes to rest before eating.

Frozen fish 140g — no timing recorded.
