#tuna

**Summary:** Yellowfin, marinated, seared very briefly.

Related: [[Cooking techniques]] · [[Ingredients, brands and tools]] · [[Oil and cooking]] · [[wiki/reference/food/techniques/Salmon|Salmon]] · [[Ingredients, brands and tools#Canned fish|Canned fish]]

Yellowfin is medium in terms of mercury and has good taste.

# Marinade

chilli pepper · sesame seed oil · half a bottle of sweet chilli sauce · ginger · Chinese 5 spice · 2 limes/lemons · tuna steak.

Chop up and mix all ingredients.

# Cooking

Get the pan very hot — 14 was too hot. Cook no more than 30-45s each side.

Sides: frozen veg, jacket potato.
