#airfryer #chicken

**Summary:** The user's own timing log for Birds Eye frozen chicken and Kievs, including the attempts that failed. Temperatures are measured, so the misses are as useful as the hits.

Related: [[Cooking techniques]] · [[Ingredients, brands and tools]] · [[Oil and cooking]] · [[Fish fingers]]

# Breasts with veg

2 breasts for 4 min, then add frozen veg and run for another 4 min. Add garlic/ginger to a bowl with olive oil, balsamic vinegar, salt and pepper — add the veg to that to coat.

# Pan crust

At 6 min was too much: a bit dry, temp ~100°C. Maybe try 4min30s but measure temp.

With frozen veg on the outside: 4:30 wasn't long enough, 8:15 was.

# Chicken Kiev

10 min was way overcooked; the bottom completely burnt and the tip burnt as well, temp was ~90°C, so maybe 7-8 min.

Small Kiev: 5 min was too short; **6 min was good**, though the middle was only around 60°C, so give it a few minutes to rest before eating. The user has confirmed 6 minutes plus a rest works.
