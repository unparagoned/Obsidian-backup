#salmon

**Summary:** Pan method plus which type to buy.

Related: [[Cooking techniques]] · [[Ingredients, brands and tools]] · [[Oil and cooking]] · [[wiki/reference/food/techniques/Tuna steak|Tuna steak]] · [[Ingredients, brands and tools#Canned fish|Canned fish]]

# Method

Source: "I Tested 4 Salmon Types, Only One Wins" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZTvUsHzf2o

- Oil and salt all over
- Score three lines down the skin side
- Cook on medium-low heat around 3 min till it's a third of the way through
- Flip, turn heat to low, add butter and baste

# Which type

Scottish and Atlantic are good. King is good as well but very soft.
