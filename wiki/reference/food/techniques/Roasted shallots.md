#onions

**Summary:** From the user's `Onions` note. Fry, oven, then caramelise with sugar and baste in stock.

Related: [[Cooking techniques]] · [[Ingredients, brands and tools]] · [[Oil and cooking]] · [[Vegetables]]

https://www.youtube.com/shorts/efQgC4jI2Is

Halve. Fry for a bit. Oven at 200°C till soft. Sprinkle caster sugar on the cut side. Fry till caramelised. Add butter, flip, add stock. Baste with stock; add thyme, salt and pepper.
