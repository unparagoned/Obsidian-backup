#vegetables

**Summary:** Per-vegetable methods. The recurring pattern is brown hard first, then finish gently — steam, oven or stock.

Related: [[Cooking techniques]] · [[Ingredients, brands and tools]] · [[Oil and cooking]] · [[Roasted shallots]] · [[Lentils]]

Source: "7 Techniques to INSTANTLY Upgrade Your Vegetables" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a8mRGkGlLJA

- **Carrots** #carrots — cook in stock and honey, then when reduced add butter to emulsify.
- **Brussels sprouts** #brusselsprouts — high temperature to brown with a lot of oil, then cover and steam on low; add lemon then parmesan.
- **Cauliflower** #cauliflower — high temp to brown, then into the oven.
- **Beetroot** #beetroot — lots of oil and salt on whole beets, wrap in a tinfoil package, then oven; take the peel off with kitchen roll, cut up, add olive oil, balsamic vinegar and salt.
- **Kale** #kale — high temp and lots of oil, add garlic, add chicken stock with the lid on.
