# Is oily, fishy hake normal?

No. Hake is a lean white fish in the cod family, with a mild flavour, so oiliness and a strong fish smell point to the cooking or the fish's freshness, not the species. *Likely:* oil soaked in from a fryer that was too cool or oil that was old; a fishy smell suggests fish that was not fresh, since fresh white fish smells of very little.

>Hake belongs to the group of white or lean fish for its low contribution of fats ... You will only consume 0.7 grams in each 100 grams serving.
