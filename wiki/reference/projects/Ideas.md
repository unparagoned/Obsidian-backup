# Objectively good films/shows

I think if you set what kind of factors you are interested in, then with arguments on either side a LLM could judge them and say if a film was objectively good based on those kinds of factors.

e.g. Finale of dare devil was all based on the final legal scene, but none of the legal scene made sense either in terms of real life or even in terms of how the legal system in DD was setup. It wasn't clever or anything, it was just poor writing/luck to get them out of the situation. A good show would have a clever ending. 

Many shows rated well have a terrible storyline or just don't do anything interesting. Nomadland has nothing interesting or good in really. None of the reviews made it seem good. The explanations people gave was simply that it had good visuals. But they didn't seem any better than breaking bad or anything else in that region. 

A benefit, would be that this idea is very controversial, which would be good for advertising. 

# API terminal tool

e.g. connect to an online tool to convert markdown to pdf. 

Maybe the online tool could be hosted in docker or something locked down.
