#cleaning

**Summary:** Laundry stripping method for sheets and towels — soak in hot water with borax, washing soda and detergent (1:1:2), then rinse on a water-only cycle.

Related: [[Household and admin]] · [[Chemicals and microplastics]]

How to clean sheets:

1. Gather some just-washed laundry, either wet or dry. You can strip wash the laundry either in a clean bathtub, a large bucket, or a top-loading washing machine.
2. Start by filling the tub with very hot water. Then add a mixture of borax, washing soda, and laundry detergent, following a 1 to 1 to 2 ratio, adjusting the amounts based on how much water you're using. For a tub full, try about 1/4 cup borax, 1/4 cup washing soda, and 1/2 cup detergent.
3. Once the mixture has dissolved, add your laundry. Let the fabric soak until the water has cooled, about 4 hours, stirring occasionally.
4. Wring out the fabric as much as possible, then wash the items once more in the washing machine on a water-only cycle.
