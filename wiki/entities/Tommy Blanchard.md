**The user's assessment:** "Seems very good. Has good neuroscience understanding and it links into materialism."

- The biology of colour vision, like asymmetries, means that my red is your red. It's not possible my red is your green. Red has a relationship with other colours which kind of fixes it.
- There are specific parts of the brain around detecting movement. So some people can see an object in various places but can't see movement. You wouldn't think it was possible.
