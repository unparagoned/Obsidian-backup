# Protein and muscle

Source: "Stuart Phillips, PhD, on Building Muscle with Resistance Exercise and Reassessing Protein Intake" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r8DSpOd0Nzc

Most important is exercise. Then protein intake over the day; timing and source is less important. Omega 3 prevents muscle wasting.

# Exercise, sauna and cold

Source: "Maximizing Healthspan with Exercise, Sauna, & Cold Exposure" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OCddz_0NrEE

Vigorous exercise has mental performance benefits.

Norwegian 4x4 reversed the heart age of people in their 50s by 20 years after just 2 years of exercise; larger/stronger and more flexible. Also on [[Exercise]].

Lactate is a messenger, activates various pathways and signals BDNF.

The brain works hard when you are exercising; norepinephrine increases focus, etc. Vigorous exercise results in more BDNF.

Sauna improves cardiovascular health, blood pressure, etc. Needed to be in there for 20min x2 a week. Infrared saunas don't show the same benefits, probably because they don't get as hot, but maybe could get some benefits if staying for 40min plus. Hot baths also have benefits.

Heat-shock proteins keep proteins in the right shape; it protects against amyloids.

Exercise and heat cause some acute "good" inflammation, but the body's reaction means you have lower overall inflammation.

Cold exposure increases brown fat and impairs muscle growth. Increases adrenaline in the body and brain. #coldexposure Increased mitochondria in muscle, so can have benefits in cardio sports. See [[Cold exposure]].

The evidence around fatty-15 is fairly weak. Maybe it's good but not much good evidence.

Magnesium citrate, malate, glycinate. Magnesium glycinate — both parts help with sleep. #magnesium
