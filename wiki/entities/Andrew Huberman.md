**Summary:** Stanford neuroscientist and podcaster; the source of a large share of the sleep, supplement and hormone material in this wiki, usually via his guests ([[Kyle Gillett]], [[Matthew Walker]], [[Gina Poe]]).

Related: [[Sleep]] · [[Night owls and bimodal sleep]] · [[Caffeine - Coffee]] · [[Cold exposure]] · [[Kyle Gillett]] · [[Matthew Walker]]

Is religious, he talks about prayer practice: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hlOA8ObQJXo

Huberman Lab #80, "Optimize & Control Your Brain Chemistry to Improve Health & Performance": https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T65RDBiB5Hs

Studies show that moving the sleep schedule of people who think they are night owls forward had lots of health benefits. So 2-3h earlier. See [[Night owls and bimodal sleep]].

- Eat at the same time.
- No coffee after 3pm.
- No naps after 4pm; no naps over 90min.
- Exercise in the morning.
- Cold 1-10min — see [[Cold exposure]].

Neuromodulator shorthand from the same episode:

- Dopamine — drive
- Norepinephrine — energy
- Serotonin — feeling of contentment
- Acetylcholine — focus/learning
