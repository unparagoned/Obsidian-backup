# The sweetener video

The user's account: he did a video on a study about sweeteners. The study showed that in humans certain ones had a negative impact on the glycemic response; they then causally linked that to the gut microbiome and implanted the gut microbiome from humans into mice and showed that it impaired the glycemic response in the mice.

He said that it was probably the placebo effect for the change in glycemic response, even though they had controls and other sweeteners that didn't show that effect. Also, how does the placebo effect explain the mice having an impaired glycemic response when the mice didn't even consume the sweeteners. Then he said that the change in gut microbiome isn't necessarily bad, but that doesn't take into account how it had a negative impact on the glycemic response in the mice.

It seems like he obviously didn't actually read the whole paper. And he shits on others for not reading the actual paper and has the catchphrase about human randomised control trials. But here you have one of the best human randomised control trials possible (even he admitted that), and he ignored it.

The video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q1unt2LwFwk

The study (Cell 2022 RCT, the same one used on [[Sweetener]]): https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674(22)00919-9
