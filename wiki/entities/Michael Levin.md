# What he actually claims about slime mould

He does say it. The definition is functional and deliberately broad — sense, retain, choose, pursue a goal by varied means — and explicitly does not require neurons or imply human-like experience.

> Cognition is all around you. It really is everywhere...not just for furry, brainy things.
https://www.harvardmagazine.com/2021/10/right-now-can-slime-molds-think

He is a co-author on the position paper making the case: Vallverdú et al., "Slime mould: The fundamental mechanisms of biological cognition", *BioSystems* 165 (2018) 57–70 — a review, not an experiment.

# On "a lot of it is just physics"

The user is right that the headline evidence dissolves under scrutiny. Adamatzky (2017) showed maze-solving prototypes "simply trace flow currents or chemical diffusion gradients" — https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.04681 — which kills the maze test as evidence of intelligence. Detail on [[Basal cognition#It's not true that maze-solving shows intelligence]].

**But the debunker is Levin's co-author.** Andrew Adamatzky is on the *BioSystems* minimal-cognition paper with Levin. This is not a case of Levin defending a claim against an outside critic who dismantled it; the same people hold both positions.

# On "didn't handle the criticism right"

His answer is on the record and is not evasive — he grants the physics and denies that granting it settles anything.

> Right, aspects of physics and materials properties underlie intelligence (as is well-understood and exploited by the field of morphological computing and others). Is the claim in that video that "real intelligence" has to mean there's no physics underneath?
https://thoughtforms.life/qa-from-the-internet-and-recent-presentations-3/ (29 August 2025)

Whether that counts as handling the criticism depends on the objection being made. Against "the mechanism is physical, therefore not intelligence" it lands. Against "your definition is now so broad it doesn't discriminate" it does not engage — and that second objection is the stronger one, though no published critic pressing it has been found yet.

# On "physicalism is dead"

Not sourced. No interview, talk or paper making that claim has been located, and nothing in the material read so far supports it. Left as the user's recollection only. See [[Physicalism]].
