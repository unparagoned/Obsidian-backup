**Summary:** "Dr. Josef" is the YouTube channel name of Josef Witt-Doerring, a board-certified psychiatrist and former FDA medical officer who now runs TaperClinic, a private psychiatric-drug tapering service. He is a prominent critic of SSRIs and psychiatric drug labelling.

## Positions / claims relevant to this vault

Argues that the evidence for SSRI efficacy is weaker than commonly presented, and that the FDA's approval and labelling process understates harms; this is the thrust of the video cited in [[Depression#SSRIs]]. Video content is a YouTube opinion source, not primary evidence; the underlying meta-analyses should be checked directly.

## Credibility notes

Credentials: MD (University of Queensland, per the Psychiatry Redefined bio), psychiatry residency at Baylor, board-certified psychiatrist, former FDA medical officer. The FDA insider framing is accurate but the role was reviewer-level, not leadership.

Conflict of interest: he owns and profits from a tapering business that sells the service his videos argue for. The vault's framing in [[Depression#SSRIs]] ("someone who worked for the FDA") omits this. News article (Slate, Molly Olmstead, 25 July 2025) on the FDA's July 2025 SSRIs-and-pregnancy panel he sat on:

>the owner of a private 'tapering clinic' that charges patients tens of thousands of dollars to guide them through quitting psychiatric medications, asserting women sometimes struggle for years to come off antidepressants
>https://slate.com/news-and-politics/2025/07/fda-rfk-ssris-panel-depression-pregnancy.html

A separate critique of the same panel (GoozNews substack, newsletter):

>9 out of 10 panelists have either been paid witnesses in litigation involving antidepressants, run media platforms rooted in SSRI skepticism, or have published research pointing to the drugs' potential risks in developing babies.
>https://gooznews.substack.com/p/fda-advisory-committee-system-on
