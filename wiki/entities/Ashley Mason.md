#ashleymason #sleep

**Summary:** Sleep clinician; the user's main source on CBT-I. Mixed verdict because she accepts the night-owl chronotype framing the user rejects.

**The user's assessment:** "Seems ok but believes the whole night owl stuff. Some good but feels a bit meh."

Related: [[Sleep]] · [[Night owls and bimodal sleep]] · [[Sleep - circadian schedule]] · [[Caffeine - Coffee]] · [[Ashwagandha(Withania somnifera)]] · [[Magnesium]]

Source: YouTube interview, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NneZHMv7VBU

![[raw/assets/6b6f69025cc2967cb7bc1ab1f71d9f67_MD5.png]]

CBT-I components as she describes them:

- Associate sleep with bed, so only sleep in bed.
- Time in bed restriction — so in bed just for sleep. Maybe down to 5.5 hours.
- Untrue statements — identify negative thoughts and behaviours.
- Muscle relaxation.

CBT-I has over 50% success rate, which is really high, and those that fail are mainly those that can't adhere to it.

Electric blanket usage was linked to cancer, so might be linked to impairing circadian rhythm through temp.

Stop coffee before 11. Decaf coffee includes caffeine, so should cut off with all coffee before 11.

People like mindfulness and body scan stuff, but it doesn't seem to actually improve sleep.

Evidence for melatonin and ashwagandha is weak. She doesn't use them in her patients.

Beta blockers inhibit melatonin and can cause insomnia. In which case 0.5mg melatonin can help.

She says to wake up at the same time every day, rather than go to sleep at the same time each day. Since it's easy to fix the wakeup, rather than force the sleep time. The user doesn't like this — see [[Michael Breus]], who says the same, and [[Sleep - circadian schedule]].
