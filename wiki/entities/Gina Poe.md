#ginapoe

**Summary:** Sleep neuroscientist; source of the claim that early sleep stages are clock-driven, so a late bedtime means missing them outright rather than shifting them.

Related: [[Sleep]] · [[Night owls and bimodal sleep]] · [[Sleep - circadian schedule]] · [[Matthew Walker]] · [[Andrew Huberman]]

Source: "Dr. Gina Poe: Use Sleep to Enhance Learning, Memory & Emotional State | Huberman Lab Podcast" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BMTt8gSl13s

The earlier stages of sleep are based on time, so if you go to sleep late you just miss them. So hormone releases, etc.
