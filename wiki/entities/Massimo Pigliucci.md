#massimopigliucci #stoicism

**Summary:** Philosopher and modern Stoic writer; source of the practical Stoic moves the user keeps.

Related: [[Stoicism]] · [[Stoicism#Stoics are compatibilists]] · [[Mental attitude]] · [[Habits]]

Source: "5 Stoic lessons to become your best self in 2026" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4EmuBcIlWBs

If someone insults you:

- Maybe they are right and you should accept it, so no need to get mad.
- Or the person is wrong and that's his problem not yours. So no reason to get mad.

Reflect but focus on the future, with goals. e.g. Moving forward I want to do x more and y less.

Focus on what's in your control.
