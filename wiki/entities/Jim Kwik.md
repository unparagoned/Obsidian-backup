**Summary:** Memory-technique speaker. Two of the claims recorded here (cross-lateral movement requiring "cross brain communication") are pop-neuroscience.

Related: [[Memory]] · [[Justin Sung]] · [[Exercise]]

Source: "The Memory Expert: Do You Want A Perfect Memory? WATCH." — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1cRmjpBBn7s

- Do exercises or drills that cross the centre line (left hand to right knee), requiring cross brain communication.
- If you fight (talk about) for your limitations you get to keep them.
- Using multiple senses helps with memory.
- The key to comprehension is asking questions.

**Evidence:** *Weak evidence* — motivational-speaker content, no studies cited. The cross-lateral "brain communication" claim in particular is the Brain Gym idea, which has been repeatedly debunked.
