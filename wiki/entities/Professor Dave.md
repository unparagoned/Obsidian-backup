#professordave

**Summary:** Science-communication YouTuber known for debunking videos. The user's note is about his conduct toward critics rather than his content, sourced to a Reddit discussion thread.

Related: [[Eric Weinstein]] · [[Reddit]] · [[Isreal]]

Reddit comment (r/DecodingTheGurus) on the quality of his criticism.

>Yeah, Dave is a pretty terrible YouTube personality, even if he criticizes people who deserve to be criticized. He is arrogant and demeaning even when he is wrong, in subjects where he obviously does not have much of a clue. I forget if it was Eric Weinstein or some other physics video, his criticism of the math/physics was actually factually totally off. Uninformed criticism like that makes people like Eric and Sabine who actually know some math and physics look good in comparison.
>https://www.reddit.com/r/DecodingTheGurus/comments/1noplg6/comment/nfuafms/

The thread that comment came from, saved in full in the original note: a user ("Shaggy") describes watching Professor Dave's debunking videos, then finding his replies to critics in the comments section — quoting two exchanges where he calls a commenter a "brainwashed moron" and refers to "shitting on worthless trolls who deserve much worse". The post asks where the line is between resisting bad arguments and mean-spirited squabbling.

>Commenter: "Dave resorts to ad hominems when his scientific arguments fail"
>Dave: "Hey look the brainwashed moron doesn't know what ad hominem means and is pretending my arguments 'failed' because he's allergic to reality. How cute"

>Commenter: "I have always liked, agreed and been educated by Dave, but am a bit surprised that he has lowered himself to making the derogatory comments below. Very surprised indeed."
>Dave: "Which "derogatory comments", sweetie? You mean shitting on worthless trolls who deserve much worse?"
