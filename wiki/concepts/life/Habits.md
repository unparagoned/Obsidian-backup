**Summary:** The user's habit stacks, built on the *Atomic Habits* method: each new behaviour is anchored to an existing one, plus cue-based rules for stairs, exercise snacks and takeaways, and a spoken script for talking themselves out of drinking.

**Evidence:** Uncited personal practice — the method comes from [[Atomic habits]]; nothing here is a claim about efficacy.

Related: [[Atomic habits]] · [[James Clear]] · [[Routine]] · [[Alcohol]] · [[Exercise]] · [[Gym]]

# Stack

- After waking up I will measure my weight
- After weighing put on gym clothes
- After putting on gym clothes go to the gym

- After 19:00 take sleeping supplements
- After sleeping supplements stretch for 10 minutes
- After stretching play guitar for 10 minutes

# Cues

- When I see or can take stairs, take the stairs
- After bathroom do exercise snacks
- After refilling drink do exercise snacks
- Before having takeaway think about what I can make

# Drinking script

Think about drinking — point out and say: makes you fat, damages your brain, damages your body, costs money, impairs sleep, impairs learning, impairs learning guitar.

See [[Alcohol]] for what the evidence actually supports here.
