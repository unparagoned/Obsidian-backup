**Summary:** Short self-management rules: an evening reflection triad, speak slowly to sound more intelligent, action creates motivation (start with a minute), frame behaviour as identity not goal, and the 10-10-10 rule for decisions.

**Evidence:** *Weak evidence* — TikTok sources. The identity-framing idea comes from [[Atomic habits]].

Related: [[Habits]] · [[Routine]] · [[Mental attitude]] · [[Self Control]] · [[Atomic habits]]

Evening reflection:

- Think about your favourite part of the day
- What were you proud of during the day
- What are you looking forward to tomorrow

Speak slowly — people think you are more intelligent. https://vm.tiktok.com/ZN8JCbEFN/

Action creates motivation. So just start, or plan to do a minute.

Frame things as identity rather than goal. E.g. "I'm someone who exercises each day" vs "exercise daily". https://vm.tiktok.com/ZN8eQYndr/

# Decision making

10-10-10 rule. Think about how you will feel in 10 minutes, 10 weeks, 10 years. Will you wish you did it, or regret it.
