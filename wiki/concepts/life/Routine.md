# Morning

- 05:05 Weigh
- 05:10 Brush teeth
- 05:15 Gym
- +0h Back from gym
- +1min Clean ears
- +5min Coffee (caffeinated) when back from gym
- +10min Aerate sea monkeys
- +15min Squats looking at sun
- +30min Shower
- +40min Lips
- +1h Finish [[Essential Amino Acids(EAA)]], [[Creatine]], [[Citrulline malate]] — 1h after gym
- 11:00 Open blinds (when the sun no longer shines directly in eyes)

**Avoid:** don't have more than one caffeinated coffee; if you want more have decaf. See [[Caffeine - Coffee]].

- Cook lunch
- +0h Finish eating lunch

- +15min Lips

- 18:00 Clear inbox
- 18:05 Review tasks
- 19:20 Guitar
- 19:35 Stretch/meditation
- 19:50 Sleep supplements — [[Sleep#Supplements]]
- 19:55 Brush teeth
- 19:59 Lips
- 20:00 Memory training — [[Memory os]]

- 21:00 TikTok

# Whole day

Squats or burpees every hour.

**Avoid:** alcohol (only when socialising with others); pre-workout and similar.
