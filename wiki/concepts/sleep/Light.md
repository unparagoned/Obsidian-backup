#light

**Summary:** Light at night in older adults is associated with obesity, diabetes and hypertension (cross-sectional actigraphy cohort). The main light material lives under [[Sleep#Light]] and [[Night owls and bimodal sleep#Light exposure]].

Related: [[Sleep#Light]] · [[Night owls and bimodal sleep#Light exposure]] · [[Sleep - circadian schedule]] · [[Dementia alzheimer#Sleep]]

[[Night owls and bimodal sleep#Light exposure]]

Cross-sectional cohort of older adults with wrist actigraphy (SLEEP, 2022); association only, reverse causation (ill people leaving lights on) not excluded.

>Light at night in older age is associated with obesity, diabetes, and hypertension
