**Summary:** The note collects cases where "natural" and "artificial" versions of a thing differ in effect, so naturalness itself is not the relevant variable. Current example: ruminant (dairy) trans fats show no cardiometabolic harm, unlike industrial trans fats.

Related: [[Ultra processed foods(UPF)]] · [[Sweetener]] · [[Food preservatives]] · [[Meat]] · [[Chemicals and microplastics]] · [[Paracetamol]]

Natural transfats don't have negative effects like artificial transfats.

Systematic review (Clinical Biochemistry 2026) of dairy trans fatty acids and cardiometabolic outcomes; design of included studies not stated in the note.

>Consumption of TFA (1.3–13.2 g/d) from different types of dairy foods was not linked to adverse effects on cardiometabolic health.
>https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0271531726000357?via%3Dihub
