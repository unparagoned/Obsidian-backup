# Idealism

What about experiments that give you a fake hand, you experience stuff as if that fake hand was real. If things were really consciousness at the fundamental layer, the consciousness of the fake hand would be completely different. So you have to accept that consciousness at the fundamental layer is subject to illusions, etc.
