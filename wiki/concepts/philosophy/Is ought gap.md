**Summary:** Stub, framed as an analogy rather than a treatment of Hume's gap: if evolution had made every human prefer red, that still wouldn't make red objectively the best colour.

Related: [[Objective art]] · [[Free will#Justice]] · [[Claims and testimony]]

One example I think cosmic skeptic said. Is there an objective best colour? We'd say no. But what if say in the past our evolutionary tree something happened that meant all humans like the colour red. Would that make "red" objectively the best colour?
