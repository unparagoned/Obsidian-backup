**Summary:** Stub. Against the "physics only tells you how things behave, not what they are" objection: our contact with a person is also only a model, and we still identify the model with the person.

Related: [[Physicalism]] · [[Consciousness]] · [[Claims and testimony]]

There is an argument that physics just tells you how stuff acts not what it actually is. But when say you interact with a person, it's really just a model in your head, you don't know about the actual underlying reality. But we reasonably identify your model with the person.
