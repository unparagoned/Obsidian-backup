#exercise

**Summary:** Lunge backwards rather than forwards to reduce knee loading, stepping back and slightly out for balance. Technique from a YouTube video.

Related: [[Gym]] · [[Exercise]]

- Lung backwards, forwards puts too much force on the knee
- Step back and out for balance and stability
