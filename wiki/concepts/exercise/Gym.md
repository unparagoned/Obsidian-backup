# Set types

- **Heavy** — two ramp sets (e.g. 50% ×6, 75% ×3), then 1-2 sets of 6-10 at ~75-85% 1RM, stopping 1-2 reps short of failure. See [[High-rep training#Alternating heavy and light sessions]].
- **Light** — 1-2 sets of 12-25 to failure, straight in, no warm-up. See [[High-rep training#Does low load give the same muscle growth?]].
- **Myo** — one activation set of 15-20 to ~1 rep in reserve, then 3-5 breaths' rest and mini-sets of 3-5 reps until the count drops (usually 3-4 mini-sets). Replaces two light sets. See [[High-rep training#One myo-rep set vs two standard sets]].

Heavy option only on lunge, bench press, shoulder press and cable pull-through. Each lift is heavy in one session of its pair and light in the other, so every session has one heavy lift.
