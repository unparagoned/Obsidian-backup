#self-control

**Summary:** Two longitudinal studies found that earlier well-being predicted later self-control (at 1 and 6 months), but self-control did not predict later well-being. The direction runs from well-being to self-control, not the reverse.

Related: [[Mental health]] · [[Mental attitude]] · [[Depression]] · [[Night owls and bimodal sleep#Self control linked to chronotype]]

Primary paper, two longitudinal studies (1-month and 6-month follow-up), correlational not experimental.

>We found that earlier levels of well-being positively predicted levels of self-control 1 month (Study 2) and 6 months (Study 1) later. However, self-control did not predict later well-being.
https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/19485506251385007
