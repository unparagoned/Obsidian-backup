#psychedelics

**Summary:** A 2025 systematic review/meta-analysis found psilocybin may reduce depressive symptoms in cancer patients, with mixed effects on anxiety and time-dependent gains in spiritual well-being and quality of life.

**Evidence:** *Some evidence* — meta-analysis of small trials, cancer patients only.

Related: [[Depression]] · [[Depression#MDMA]] · [[Mental health]]

Systematic review/meta-analysis (BMC Psychology 2025) of psilocybin trials in cancer patients; quality-of-life finding comes from single-arm data only.

>Psilocybin may reduce depressive symptoms in cancer patients, with mixed effects on anxiety and time-dependent improvements in spiritual well-being and (in single-arm data) quality of life.
