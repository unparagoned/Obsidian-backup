#anthropic

**Summary:** Stub. Three reasons three spatial dimensions are special: the Standard Model is only renormalisable there, stable orbits need it, and chaos is impossible below it and excessive above it.

**Evidence:** *No real evidence* cited — assertions with no sources.

Related: [[Relativity]] · [[Quantum Mechanics]] · [[Simulation hypothesis]]

Renamed from `Anthropic.md` to avoid colliding with Anthropic the company in the AI pages.

The standard model is only renormalisable in D=3.

There can be no orbits with D != 3.

You can't get chaos with continuous values in D<=2, but there is too much chaos in D>3.
