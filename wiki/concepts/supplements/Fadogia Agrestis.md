#fadogiaagrestis 

**Summary:** Fadogia agrestis is promoted for testosterone but the data is mostly animal, with no real human evidence, and it is toxic at higher doses. The dosing/cycling scheme recorded comes from Kyle Gillett.
**Evidence:** None in humans; toxicity at higher doses. Dose: 300 mg (called safe); maybe 600 mg every other day; 1 month on, 1 month off.
Related: [[Testosterone supplements]], [[Tongkat Ali]], [[Supplements]]

> [!warning] Conflicting evidence
> This page records a 300 mg cycled protocol (Kyle Gillett); [[Testosterone supplements]] lists Fadogia under "No / Maybe not". No human trials either way.
