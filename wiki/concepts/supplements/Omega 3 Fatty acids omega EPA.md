#omega3 

**Summary:** Omega-3 (EPA/DHA, not plant ALA) is taken for mental health and general health. The user's rules: over 1000 mg EPA/day (maybe over 4 g/day total), triglyceride form not ethyl ester, dark bottle, keep in the fridge, buy big brands in small quantities so it does not go rancid, prefer small-fish sources, avoid krill oil. Peter Attia and the user favour Carlson.
**Evidence:** Some evidence (mental health, one trial cited); Mixed evidence (prostate cancer concern, per Bill Harris). Dose: >1000 mg EPA/day; maybe >4 g/day.
