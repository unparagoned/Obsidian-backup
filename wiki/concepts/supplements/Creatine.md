**Summary:** Creatine monohydrate is the user's most-evidenced supplement, for exercise energy, muscle, and mental performance and health. The note says higher doses (20 g/day) are used for cognition than for muscle, and it is best taken after exercise.
**Evidence:** Strong evidence (exercise); Some evidence (cognition, mental health). Dose: 20 g/day for mental performance/cognition (note); muscle dose not stated in note.
Related: [[Exercise]], [[Gym]], [[Depression]], [[Mental health]], [[Testosterone supplements]], [[Protein]], [[Alpha-gpc choline]]

- Loads of benefits for exercise and mental performance/health. Most evidenced.
- 20g a day for mental performance, it's more than you take for just muscles

- Probably best after exercise. Some stomach discomfort if taken before exercise.

- Generally recommended and safe
-  Helps give you more energy for exercise and the mind
- Helps with testosterone
- Helps with mental health
- May make mania worse
- Good even when dieting

Hcl may or may not be better. Creatine monohydrate has been widely tested so just use that.
