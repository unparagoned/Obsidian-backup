#citrullinemalate

**Summary:** Citrulline malate raises arginine and nitric oxide more effectively than arginine itself, and is taken for exercise performance, blood flow and blood pressure. A meta-analysis found diastolic BP reduction at doses of 6 g/day or more.
**Evidence:** Some evidence. Dose: 6-18 g/day citrulline malate, split into 3-4 doses with one pre-workout (note: 6 g 3x a day).
Related: [[Exercise]], [[Gym]], [[Heart]], [[Testosterone supplements]], [[C Vitamin]], [[Bio-pycnogenol]]


- 6g 3x a day ~18g Citrulline malate
- Have with an anti-oxidant like vitamin C - the nitric oxide will get recycled


The best dose to take in a 24 hour period is 6-18 grams. 6 grams is the minimal amount needed to notice any effects, and doses taken at 18 grams per day had extremely noticeable effects. The best way to do this is to split doses into 4 different times and one of those times should be pre-workout.

From <[https://us.myprotein.com/thezone/supplements/citrulline-malate-benefits-dosage-when-to-take/](https://us.myprotein.com/thezone/supplements/citrulline-malate-benefits-dosage-when-to-take/)>

Interestingly, consuming citrulline may increase arginine in the body more than consuming arginine itself ([4Trusted Source](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/27749691)).

This is because of differences in how the body processes and absorbs arginine and citrulline ([2Trusted Source](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/23022123)).

The increase in nitric oxide and blood flow may be one of the processes involved in citrulline's beneficial effects on exercise performance.

Although citrulline isn't an amino acid used directly to build proteins, it has been shown to increase protein synthesis by stimulating an important signaling pathway involved in muscle building ([2Trusted Source](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/23022123), [7Trusted Source](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pubmed/24972455)).

From <[https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/citrulline-supplements#section2](https://www.healthline.com/nutrition/citrulline-supplements#section2)>

The subgroup analysis showed a significant diastolic BP reduction in studies that used doses of ≥6 g/day
