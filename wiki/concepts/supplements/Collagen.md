**Summary:** A systematic review/meta-analysis of 23 RCTs (n=1474) found no clinical evidence that collagen supplements prevent or treat skin aging.
**Evidence:** No real evidence (for skin aging). Dose: not given in note.
Related: [[Protein]], [[Supplements]]

Systematic review and meta-analysis of 23 RCTs (American Journal of Medicine, 2025) on collagen for skin aging:
>A total of 23 RCTs with 1474 participants were analyzed...There is currently no clinical evidence to support the use of collagen supplements to prevent or treat skin aging.
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0002934325002839
