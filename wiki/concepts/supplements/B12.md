**Summary:** Notes on which form of B12 to take. Cyanocobalamin is cheaper, more stable and the form used in most studies; methylcobalamin lacks enough data.
**Evidence:** not graded in note. Dose: not given in note.

# Notes

- Cyanocobalamin is the cheaper one, more stable and had all the studies done on it.
- It's not clear Methylcobalamin works - not enough data.

Source: YouTube (Dr Michael Greger, NutritionFacts).
