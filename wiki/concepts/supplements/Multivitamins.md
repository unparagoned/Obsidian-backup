**Summary:** The COSMOS trial (RCT in older adults) found daily multivitamin-mineral supplementation improved episodic memory over 2 years, and a meta-analysis of the COSMOS cognitive substudies found benefits for global cognition and episodic memory.
**Evidence:** Good evidence, modest effect (older adults). Dose: daily multivitamin-mineral; product not specified in note.
Related: [[Dementia alzheimer]], [[Supplements]]

RCT shows that multivitamins have benefits of memory and cognition.

RCT (COSMOS-Clinic, older adults, 2 years) plus meta-analysis of the three COSMOS cognitive substudies (PubMed):
>In COSMOS-Clinic, daily MVM supplementation leads to a significantly more favorable 2-y change in episodic memory. The meta-analysis within COSMOS cognitive substudies indicates that daily MVM significantly benefits both global cognition and episodic memory. These findings within the COSMOS trial support the benefits of a daily MVM in preventing cognitive decline among older adults.
>https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38244989/
