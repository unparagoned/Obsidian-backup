**Summary:** Where MSG actually helps: foods that already contain inosinate or guanylate — soups, stews, roasted (not delicate) vegetables, meat, spicy food. A 75% salt / 25% MSG ratio worked well on eggs.

**Evidence:** *Weak evidence* — one YouTube explainer; no studies cited. The synergy claim (glutamate + inosinate/guanylate) is standard food science.

Related: [[Cooking techniques]] · [[Naturalistic fallacy]] · [[Ultra processed foods(UPF)]]

Source: "Does MSG actually make food taste better?" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCa43F2NqnY

Acts well with some foods, interacting with the chemicals in them:

- Soup, stew
- Roasted veg, but not the lighter veg
- Anything with inosinate or guanylate
- Meat
- Spicy foods

Eggs: 75% salt / 25% MSG was a good ratio.
