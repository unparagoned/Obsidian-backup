#resistancestarch

**Summary:** The user's takeaway: 40 g/day of resistant starch reduces visceral fat via the gut microbiome. Supported by a human crossover trial with mouse faecal-transfer experiments (Nature Metabolism 2024) and an RCT in normal-weight adults showing 40 g/day changed body fat, SCFAs, insulin and GLP-1.

**Evidence:** *Some evidence* (small RCTs plus mechanistic mouse work). Dose: 40 g/day.

Related: [[Gut microbiome]] · [[Glucose spikes]] · [[Mounjaro tirzepatide]] · [[Ultra processed foods(UPF)]]

40g of resistance starch daily helps reduce visceral fat, works via gut microbiome

Human crossover trial (8 weeks, overweight adults) plus faecal transfer into mice (Li et al., Nature Metabolism 2024):
>We demonstrate that RS can facilitate weight loss at least partially through _B._ _adolescentis_ and that the gut microbiota is essential for the action of RS.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10963277/

RCT in normal-weight adults, 40 g/day resistant starch (2019):
>These observations suggest that a daily intake of 40 g of RS is effective in modulating body fat, SCFAs, early-phase insulin and GLP-1 secretion and the gut microbiota in normal-weight subjects.
>https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30894560/
