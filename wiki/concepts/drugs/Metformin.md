#metformin

**Summary:** Metformin lowers hepatic glucose output, raises insulin sensitivity and reduces intestinal glucose absorption, but blunts the training adaptations of exercise in older adults and depletes B12. The user's verdict: not worth taking for healthy people; may help weight loss.

**Evidence:** *Good evidence, modest effect* for glucose control and weight in diabetics; *Some evidence* (RCT, older adults) that it impairs exercise gains. No dose given.

Related: [[Mounjaro tirzepatide#Combination with metformin]] · [[Exercise]] · [[B12]] · [[Glucose spikes]] · [[Dementia alzheimer#Drugs]]

Overall not great, it impairs the benefits of exercise. Could help lose weight.
