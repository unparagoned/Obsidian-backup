## Individual drugs

- [[wiki/concepts/drugs/Olanzapine|Olanzapine]] (Zyprexa) — full page. Worst of the class for weight gain and metabolic harm; Eli Lilly pleaded guilty in 2009 to marketing it off-label for dementia.
- [[wiki/concepts/drugs/Quetiapine|Quetiapine]] (Seroquel) — sleep-focused page. The one actually prescribed as a sleeping pill; the only RCT in primary insomnia was null; AstraZeneca paid $520m over marketing it for sleeplessness.

## Use for insomnia

*No real evidence* for the class in primary insomnia; VA/DoD 2025 and the European insomnia guideline 2023 both recommend against it, naming quetiapine. The metabolic and mortality costs are not worth paying for a symptom.

The two drugs differ on the one point in their favour. Olanzapine's acute effect on sleep architecture is genuinely better than most sedatives — it increases slow-wave sleep. Quetiapine does not; it adds stage 2 light sleep. And quetiapine, unlike olanzapine, has actually been tested in primary insomnia and failed. Full comparison at [[wiki/concepts/drugs/Quetiapine#How it compares with olanzapine for sleep|Quetiapine#How it compares with olanzapine for sleep]]; see also [[wiki/concepts/drugs/Olanzapine#Sleep and insomnia|Olanzapine#Sleep and insomnia]] and [[wiki/concepts/sleep/Sleep#Antipsychotics - major tranquillisers - mood stabilisers|Sleep#Antipsychotics]]. #insomnia
