#olanzapine

**Summary:** The user's note records one claim, from a YouTube video, that antipsychotics reduce brain volume and cause neuronal damage. Olanzapine is also noted on the Mounjaro page as possibly increasing food and drink desire.

**Evidence:** *Some evidence* for brain-volume reduction (Likely: the Ho et al. 2011 Archives of General Psychiatry cohort and macaque studies), but the note cites only a video. No dose given.
