**Summary:** Stub. One cross-sectional finding: family income tracks brain glucose metabolism in reward and stress regions, while education level doesn't. The paper itself flags that this is associative.

**Evidence:** *Weak evidence* — cross-sectional imaging study, explicitly non-causal, reverse causation wide open.

Related: [[Genetics and IQ]] · [[Healthy food is cheaper - obesity and behaviour]] · [[Homelessness, drug addiction and mental illness]]

Filename corrected from `Nature vs Nurtue.md`.

Primary paper (European Journal of Neuroscience).

>Family income is associated with elevated brain glucose metabolism in regions involved in reward processing and stress regulation, suggesting a potential link between current socioeconomic resources and neural activity.
>However, these findings are cross-sectional and must be interpreted as associative rather than causal. Education level does not show a significant association with brain glucose metabolism.
https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ejn.70303
