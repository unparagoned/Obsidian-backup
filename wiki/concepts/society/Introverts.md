**Summary:** Stub. The "savanna theory of happiness" finding: socialising with friends raises life satisfaction for most people, but the association reverses among the more intelligent.

**Evidence:** *Weak evidence* — one cross-sectional analysis (Kanazawa & Li, BJP 2016), an interaction effect in survey data, widely reported and widely criticised.

Related: [[Socialising]] · [[Self Control]] · [[Mental attitude]] · [[Genetics and IQ]]

Primary paper (British Journal of Psychology).

>Most people get more life satisfaction when interacting with friends but it's the opposite for more intelligent people, they have higher life satisfaction if they don't interact with friends.
https://personal.lse.ac.uk/kanazawa/pdfs/BJP2016.pdf
