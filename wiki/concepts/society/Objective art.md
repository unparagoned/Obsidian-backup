# Bad films/scams

Some films are just scams; they have people writing fake reviews and just getting people to give it good reviews. But if you look at the actual reviews you'll see that they don't give any good reasons for why they think it's good.

e.g. Nomadland was a terrible film in every way, but all the good reviews were like copy-cut reviews just saying the cinematography was good. But the cinematography wasn't any better than Breaking Bad. Never once watching this was I like, wow, the cinematography is good.
