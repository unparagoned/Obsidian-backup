**Summary:** A collection of findings on measurable sex differences: sex predicted from dental X-rays (96%) and retinal photographs (~86%), structural brain differences present at birth, a sexually dimorphic neuron cluster in the amygdala, different pain-relief pathways, different substrate metabolism, and the gender-equality paradox in academic strengths.

**Evidence:** Mixed — several primary papers (Nature Scientific Reports, PNAS, Biology of Sex Differences, AJP twin study) alongside psypost/press-release summaries. The list is assembled to support one conclusion; nothing here bears on how sex differences should be treated socially.

Related: [[Trans]] · [[DSD intersex]] · [[Genetics and IQ]] · [[Woke Nonsense]]

# Prediction from anatomy

News write-up: AI system achieves 96% accuracy in determining sex from dental X-rays — https://www.psypost.org/ai-system-achieves-96-accuracy-in-determining-sex-from-dental-x-rays/

Primary paper (Scientific Reports): predicting sex from retinal fundus photographs using automated deep learning.

>Sensitivity, specificity, positive predictive value (PPV) and accuracy (ACC) were 88.8%, 83.6%, 87.3% and 86.5%, and for external validation were 83.9%, 72.2%, 78.2% and 78.6% respectively
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-021-89743-x

# Brain and biology

Primary paper (Biology of Sex Differences) on neonatal brain structure.

>Our findings demonstrate that sex differences in brain structure are already present at birth and remain comparatively stable during early postnatal development, highlighting an important role of prenatal factors in shaping sex differences in the brain.
https://bsd.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s13293-024-00657-5

Primary paper (PNAS) on a dimorphic neuron cluster.

>In this study, we identified a dense, sexually dimorphic cluster of neurons in the posterodorsal medial amygdala (MeApd)
https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2518703123

Press release (UC San Diego): in a study of meditation for chronic lower back pain, men relieved pain by releasing endogenous opioids while women relied on non-opioid pathways — https://today.ucsd.edu/story/men-and-women-use-different-biological-systems-to-reduce-pain — related to [[Placebo]].

Review on substrate metabolism.

>Men generally favor carbohydrate metabolism, while women lean towards lipid metabolism, resulting in significant sex-based differences in energy oxidation across various metabolic states such as fasting and feeding. These differences are influenced by body composition and inherent metabolic fluxes, including increased lipolysis rates in women.
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0010482524011090

# Heritability

Twin study (American Journal of Psychiatry) showing a quantitative sex effect.

>Evidence for a quantitative sex effect was found, such that heritability was significantly greater in females (35.4%) than males (28.6%)
>The model assumes that monozygotic twins share 100% of their genes, while dizygotic twins and full siblings share 50% of their genes identical by descent. Shared environment reflects family and community experiences that render the twins and siblings more similar for the phenotype in question.
https://psychiatryonline.org/doi/10.1176/appi.ajp.20230104

# Academic strengths and attitudes

Press release (University of Turku): sex differences in academic strengths are found worldwide, and girls' relative advantage in reading and boys' in science is largest in gender-equal countries — https://www.utu.fi/en/news/press-release/gender-equity-paradox-sex-differences-in-reading-and-science-as-academic

Primary paper: testosterone rapidly increases men's emotion-based dehumanization of a conservatively dressed woman — https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S030645302400218X

Primary paper (EJSP): moral commitment to gender equality was associated with increased chances of fallaciously inferring discrimination against women from contradictory evidence — https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ejsp.3071

News write-up: testosterone spikes linked to stronger political opinions in men — https://www.psypost.org/testosterone-spikes-linked-to-stronger-political-opinions-in-men/
