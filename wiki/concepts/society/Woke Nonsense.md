**Summary:** Four examples the user collects as institutional overreach: an Imperial College microaggressions handout listing "the most qualified person should get the job" as a microaggression, a UK police force conceding sex discrimination against two male officers, an NHS genomics blog describing "potential benefits" of first-cousin marriage, and an argument for more drug users in leadership.

**Evidence:** Mixed — the Imperial document, the BBC report and the BMJ piece are all real primary or reported sources; the last item has no source.
